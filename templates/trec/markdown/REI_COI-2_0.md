**(For Informational Purposes Only)**

Upon notification from the Texas Real Estate Commission (TREC) that an applicant for an inspector license has met all other licensing requirements, the applicant shall file proof of financial responsibility with TREC. This Certificate of Insurance (Certificate) expires on the same date that the below named policy expires. An inspector renewing a license is required to file a new Certificate with TREC, no later than 15 days after this Certificate expires. This Certificate confers no rights upon the certificate holder and is issued for informational purposes only. This Certificate does not amend, extend, or alter coverage afforded by the policy specified below.
