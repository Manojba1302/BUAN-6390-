[#2 ☐]  (1) WAIVER. Buyer w aives Buyer’s right to terminate the contract under Paragraph 2B of the

Third Party Financing Addendum if Property Approval is not obtained because the opinion of value in the appraisal does not satisfy lender’s underwriting requirements.

If the lender reduces the amount of the loan due to the opinion of value, the cash portion of Sales Price is increased by the amount the loan is reduced due to the appraisal. [#3 ☐]  (2) PARTIAL WAIVER. Buyer w aives Buyer’s right to terminate the contract under Paragraph 2B

of the Third Party Financing Addendum if:

(i) Property Approval is not obtained because the opinion of value in the appraisal does

not satisfy lender’s underwriting requirements; and (ii) the opinion of value is $[____] or more. [#4 ____]

If the lender reduces the amount of the loan due to the opinion of value, the cash portion of Sales Price is increased by the amount the loan is reduced due to the appraisal.

[#5 ☐]  (3) ADDITIONAL RIGHT TO TERMINATE. In addition to Buyer’s right to terminate under

Paragraph 2B of the Third Party Financing Addendum, Buyer may terminate the contract within [#6 ____] [____] days after the Effective Date if:

(i) the appraised value, according to the appraisal obtained by Buyer’s lender, is less than $[____]; and [#7 ____]

(ii) Buyer delivers a copy of the appraisal to the Seller.
