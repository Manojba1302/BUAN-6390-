### PUBLIC INFORMATION REQUEST

All requests for public information must be submitted in writing. You may use this form, or you may submit a written request for information via email to public.information@trec.texas.gov or via regular mail to

**TREC/TALCB Public Information Request, P.O. Box 12188, Austin, Texas 78711-2188.**

**5. Exceptions to Disclosure:**

In most instances, a governmental body is required to request a decision from the Attorney General in order to withhold information from a requestor. The Attorney General will review the matter and issue a decision, usually within 45 days. However, a requestor may permit a governmental body to redact information without requesting an Attorney General decision. You are not required to agree to the redaction of any information responsive to your request, but doing so may streamline the handling of your request.
